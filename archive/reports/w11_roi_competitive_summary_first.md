## Objective

Make ROI / Competitive usable live by showing the most important ROI and competitive message first, then letting the consultant expand for proof, audit, objections, and discovery.

## Implemented

- Replaced long default blocks with two top cards:
  - `ROI most important`
  - `Competitive most important`
- Kept the value decision and talk track lead visible.
- Moved ROI audit detail behind `Why this ROI`.
- Moved competitive proof stack behind `Competitive detail and NetSuite proof stack`.
- Moved talk track, objections, and discovery behind expandable detail.

## No-Regression Points

- ROI remains auditable.
- Competitive language remains workflow-based and source-labeled.
- N/LLM remains advisory only.
- No measured savings are introduced without a baseline.
- Detail is retained; it is simply not forced into the default live view.

## Learning

The content was strong but too dense for a live demo. The top of the tab now acts like an executive value card, while the deeper material remains available when the buyer challenges the story.
